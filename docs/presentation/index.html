09 / LOG VIEWER

For your pod logs.

09:02:48 [WARN] slow query 240ms
09:02:49 [ERROR] upstream 503 ← anchor
spacer (aria-hidden) — 50% of container height

The trick

Without the spacer, scrollTop = scrollHeight − clientHeight × 0.5 always exceeds max and clamps to bottom. With a ResizeObserver-sized spacer, the anchor lands at the viewport midpoint. A programmaticScroll ref prevents the pause-on-scroll detector from misfiring when we write scrollTop ourselves.
