A VXLAN fabric resync triggered by a transient Tailscale DERP flap caused BGP sessions on three Calico nodes to churn. Cluster-intel's auto-drain contained the blast radius. No customer-facing traffic was lost.

What we think happened

At 08:42 UTC the Tailscale DERP relay on node calico-node-2bba1 dropped packets for ~11s. Calico's BGP session timer is 10s, so sessions flapped across three peers in quick succession. Each peer triggered a VXLAN FDB flush; the resulting re-learn storm spiked east-west latency.

Auto-drain kicked in at T+6m once the pod-health scorer crossed the drain threshold on the first affected node. Remaining two nodes were drained preemptively. Workloads re-scheduled cleanly onto the four healthy nodes. No customer traffic was shed thanks to the ingress tier being on separate dedicated nodes.
